# Where Young Australians Move

An interactive map of where children who lived in each Australian SA2
at 16 were living at 26, and where each SA2's 26-year-olds grew up.
Australians born 1979–1997, overall, by parental-income quartile and
by sex. Geography is ASGS 2016 SA2. In the spirit of
[migrationpatterns.org](https://migrationpatterns.org/) (Sprung-Keyser,
Hendren and Porter 2022, *The Radius of Economic Opportunity*).

> **This version runs on DUMMY DATA.** Every number on the site comes
> from a simulated gravity model and carries no information about
> Australia. It is a preview of the site, and the real tables will
> replace it once they are cleared for release.

This repository is the built site only, with no build step. It is
generated by a separate R pipeline, and `data/` holds everything the
page reads, including the release tables as CSV: a summary per SA2,
and every origin-destination link that 10 or more children made.

Boundaries: Australian Bureau of Statistics, Australian Statistical
Geography Standard (ASGS) Edition 2016, CC BY 4.0. Map drawn with
[Leaflet](https://leafletjs.com/).
